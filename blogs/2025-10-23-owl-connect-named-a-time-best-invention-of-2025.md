We’re thrilled to share that Owl Connect, Owl Labs’ wireless pairing feature, has been officially recognized by TIME as a special mention for one of The Best Inventions of 2025 . Each year, TIME celebrates innovations that are transforming the way we live, work, and connect. This recognition highlights the continued evolution of hybrid technology and the growing need for smarter, more adaptable meeting solutions.
