Hybrid work is here to stay, but the "meeting equity" gap is wider than ever. Hybrid meetings can quickly become a frustrating experience for remote participants if the technology makes communication difficult. When remote employees feel like "flies on the wall" rather than active participants, engagement drops and productivity suffers.
