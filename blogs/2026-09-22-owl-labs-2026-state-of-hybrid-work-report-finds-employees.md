Owl Labs , the first company to build AI-powered, 360-degree video conferencing solutions for hybrid organizations, today released its tenth annual State of Hybrid Work Report . Owl Labs surveyed 2,000 full-time knowledge workers in the United States to understand the latest trends and perspectives around remote, hybrid, and in-office work. The report finds that time has become the real office currency.
