Hybrid meetings have evolved far beyond simply connecting a laptop to a conference room display. As distributed teams become a permanent part of the modern workplace, organizations are looking for smarter ways to make meetings more engaging, inclusive, and productive for everyone, whether they're in the room or joining remotely. Artificial intelligence is what's making that possible.
