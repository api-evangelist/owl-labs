The modern workplace is being reshaped by its newest and fastest-growing cohort: Generation Z. Born roughly between 1997 and 2012, these digital natives are no longer just interns; they are managers, innovators, and essential contributors. As they enter the workforce in large numbers, their distinct expectations are driving an evolution in how we define and implement hybrid work .
