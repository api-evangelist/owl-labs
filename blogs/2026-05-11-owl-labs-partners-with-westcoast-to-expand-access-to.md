Today, we’re excited to announce a new UK and Ireland distribution partnership with Westcoast Limited , one of the country’s largest and most trusted IT distributors. The agreement will see Westcoast distribute the full range of Owl Labs’ award-winning Meeting Owl and Owl Bar products across the UK and Ireland, providing greater choice, availability, and dedicated support for resellers and end customers navigating the demands of hybrid work.
